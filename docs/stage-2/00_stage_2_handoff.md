## 1. Objective

Produce the **authoritative game-design specification** for Nation Supremacy: how the game works, what players can do, which rules and formulas resolve outcomes, how systems interact, and how the player experience behaves.

Stage 2 is a **design and specification stage**. It is not implementation, not a second feasibility test, and not Stage 1 resumed.

| Stage 2 is | Stage 2 is not |
|---|---|
| A rulebook, registers and formula specifications | Game code, migrations, deployments |
| Turning Stage 1's proposals into labelled rules | Re-running Stage 1 research |
| Resolving or explicitly deferring open design questions | Proving a free-tier architecture that is already evidenced |
| Specifying screens and journeys | Building the frontend |
| Numbering what needs playtesting | Pretending balance is proven |

## 7. Fixed facts to preserve

- **Name:** Nation Supremacy (D-007).
- **World:** fictional, **mapless**, symbolic network of nations and relationships.
- **Time:** one shared turn every 2 hours on fixed UTC boundaries, 12 per day.
- **Architecture direction:** Cloudflare Pages + Supabase (`pg_cron` is the only authoritative scheduler).
- **Free-first:** ₹0 required monthly cost target; measured, not guaranteed at every scale.
- **D-004 → D-015:** Operations Points — **model approved** (single military-only persistent pool, non-transferable, reserved at acceptance, consumed at resolution; **amended 2026-11-04: +1 OP at the P3.7 opening, carried below the cap**); **cap, costs and readiness scaling remain Provisional**.

Capacity is `10 + floor(infrastructure/10)` DC with weighted class costs; infrastructure is 10–500 and can never be reduced (D-069/D-070).
- **Research:** produced by institutes (1 RP each per turn), one active project per nation, 12 technologies in 5 branches, four branch-capstone programmes; effects are integer modifiers with declared caps, never percentages (D-076…D-078).
- **Recovery/backup:** forward-only turn recovery; Free has no managed backup, so daily owner-run **three-part** `db dump` (roles `--role-only`, schema default, data `--data-only`), RPO/RTO ≤24 h best-effort, quarterly CF-02 (D-025). **No backup or drill has been performed.**
- **Retention:** class-S hot 30 days → 12 monthly summaries → deleted; class-P permanent (D-026).
- **Aggregates:** k = 5 with complementary suppression (D-027).
- **Dormancy:** 14 days/168 turns; no offensive targeting, no decay, reversible archival at 12 months (D-028).
- **E-mail:** verified before nation creation; custom SMTP mandatory for public signup (D-029).
- **Accounts:** one account per person and **one active nation per account** (D-032); a shared network may restrict a **pair** until reviewed (D-033…D-037); a **reroll** retires the nation permanently and permits an immediate replacement on the same account with no gameplay cooldown (D-039).
- **Verification:** e-mail verified before nation creation (D-029); shared-network clearance by attestation and review, **never** by photographs, government ID or biometrics (D-033); there is **no** co-resident exemption (D-037).
- **Network signal:** a Cloudflare Pages Function reads `CF-Connecting-IP`, normalises it, stores only an HMAC digest; **no raw IP is stored** (D-036). Not implemented.
- **Decision policy:** ordinary design choices are made and recorded autonomously (D-014); see [AUTONOMOUS_DECISION_POLICY.md](../AUTONOMOUS_DECISION_POLICY.md).
- **Truthfulness:** the live page is a feasibility preview until gameplay exists.
