## 1. How to read this register

**IDs.** `REQ-001…REQ-039` are inherited **unchanged** from [Report 09 §12](../stage-1/09_integrated_design_synthesis_and_readiness_audit.md), which remains authoritative for their full text and their inherited source IDs. `REQ-040…REQ-047` were added by Prompt 01: constraints the Stage 1 matrix did not carry as requirements (identity, mapless world, platform delivery, the account policy, the architecture and documentation-integrity rules). `REQ-048…REQ-060` were added by Prompt 02: the foundation rules for turn identity, boundary assignment, lifecycle, phases, ordering, dual validation, reservation, cancellation windows, digest content, visibility, bounded history, recovery and the single shared world. `REQ-061…REQ-097` were added by Prompt 03: the owner model, the one-account rule, the pair mechanism and its matrix, the trusted network signal, nation creation and naming, the standard starting state, reroll, the account lifecycle, onboarding, protection, dormancy, data classes and the administration limits. `REQ-098…REQ-187` were added by Prompt 04: the 11 resources, production and power, recipes, food, treasury, inventory, actions, settlement, ledger, reporting, balance, reroll economics and the cross-system contracts. **No inherited ID was renumbered or reused**; in particular the Prompt 01 brief's phrase "REQ-01…REQ-043 owned by Prompt 03" did not reserve REQ-044…REQ-060, which already belonged to other prompts.

**Status vocabulary** (Stage 2 classification, §6 of the Prompt 01 brief):

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Approved** | Owner-stated requirement or owner-confirmed decision — fixed unless a decision changes it. A design decision made under D-014 settles the rule but does not approve the requirement |
| **Provisional** | A design exists in Stage 1 but is not approved; it may guide design work |
| **Open** | The requirement is real but the design or decision is missing |
| **Proposed** | A candidate behaviour with no design commitment yet |
| **Deferred** | Deliberately postponed behind a gate or a later stage |

**What a status does *not* mean.** A row's status is a *design* status. It says nothing about implementation or validation. **No requirement in this project has been implemented, and none has an executed validation** (Report 09 §12.3, Report 13 §5). Column "Validation" names the planned method only.

**Coverage vs validation.** Three different facts are tracked separately: the requirement *exists* (source column), its *design coverage* is assigned (Bible section + prompt), and its *validation* is planned (last column). A requirement can be approved while its design is Planned.

**Acceptance-test traceability (08C-E, Diplomacy).** For the Diplomacy requirements, the canonical method is **exhaustive bidirectional traceability**. The [workflow and acceptance companion](15_diplomacy_workflows_acceptance.md) §15.1 is the requirement summary, and its §15.5 is the authoritative relationship ledger: every test-side `REQ-` claim is classified there as **DIRECT**, **SUPPORTING** or **DEFERRED**, and every direct ledger row appears in the corresponding requirement's `Test` cell. This register owns the requirement text and status; it does not duplicate the 117 test-pair rows. `REQ-518` is the single **DEFERRED** row because Prompt 10 owns war-declaration enforcement; the other twenty rows have direct acceptance coverage. No test is executed, so a link here is planned verification, never proof of runtime behaviour.
