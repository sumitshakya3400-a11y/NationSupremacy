## 2. Finding register

Format: ID — research question — evidence — verified observation — relevance — implication — uncertainty — integrations — owner decision.

**ALR-001** — *How does P&W structure alliance ranks?* — ALS-001, ALS-013, ALS-014, ALS-017, ALS-026 — Default positions are Leader, Heir, Officer, Member and Applicant; the official manual adds High/Low/Mid Government language; live alliances use their own titles (for example Emperor, Viceroy, Archdukes); the community wiki assigns position levels 9/8/5/3. — Nation Supremacy needs a default office set but must not hard-code one constitution. — Candidate default roles; configurable names. — The manual is descriptive; the wiki is community-maintained. — AX-04. — UD-041.

**ALR-002** — *Are native permissions granular?* — ALS-001, ALS-013, ALS-017 — The community wiki lists **15** discrete permissions and says each position can carry its own set; the default positions bundle them (Leader unrestricted; Officer gets eight including Change Permissions), and custom positions can carry any subset. — The primitives are discrete but the default bundles are broad, and Change Permissions is held by default by Leader, Heir and Officer. — Model independent capabilities, roles as bundles, and separate `roles.manage`; never copy the default Officer bundle. — Community documentation; may lag the live build; no official permission matrix was found. — AX-04, AX-05. — UD-045.

**ALR-003** — *What departments do large alliances run?* — ALS-006 — Rose runs Foreign, Economic, Military, Internal, Technological and Community Affairs plus command, deputies, Speaker and council. — Department structure is alliance-specific, not a game rule. — Departments should be optional labels, not a fixed enum. — Single alliance. — AX-05. — UD-041.

**ALR-004** — *How is leadership layered?* — ALS-006 — Command tier, deputies, department heads and a council coexist. — Multi-layer governance is real practice. — Support arbitrary role nesting only if cheap; otherwise flat roles plus labels. — One alliance. — AX-05. — UD-041, UD-045.

**ALR-005** — *How are applications processed natively?* — ALS-003 — Official internal-affairs guidance describes a dedicated applicant channel, an interview, then accept/deny; the game itself holds applicant status and the Manage Applicants permission. — Interviewing is external; the native state is the application/applicant status. — Model outcomes, not conversations. — Official manual describes recommended practice, not enforcement. — AX-09. — UD-044.

**ALR-006** — *What internal-affairs work exists?* — ALS-003 — Activity checks, bank-looting avoidance (moving nations to applicant), training, MMR/infrastructure audits, removing leaks. — Alliances perform compliance work the game does not automate. — Provide rosters/readiness data, not surveillance. — Manual is guidance. — AX-12, AX-14. — UD-044.

**ALR-007** — *How does the alliance bank work?* — ALS-002, ALS-014 — A shared bank holds money and resources; Withdraw Bank moves resources to nations or other alliances; View Bank is separate; tax brackets are permissioned by Tax Brackets/Bank Access. — A shared pool is a core expectation, and view/withdraw are separate capabilities. — Segregated treasury plus ledger, no minting; split view from withdraw. — Community/official mix. — AX-06, AX-07. — UD-046.

**ALR-008** — *How is taxation modelled?* — ALS-002 — Monetary and resource percentages per bracket, auto-collected each turn; a Default bracket; bulk city-count moves; gray nations exempt. — Taxation is a P&W-specific design, not a required feature. — Do **not** copy automatic taxation without an owner decision. — Official. — AX-06. — UD-046.

**ALR-009** — *Can withdrawal rights be limited?* — ALS-002, ALS-013, ALS-016 — Withdraw Bank is a binary permission; the only bank gate is holding it. A 2015 forum proposal explicitly describes the native model as "all or nothing" and asks for tiered officer withdrawal limits. No per-actor limit or threshold is documented. — The native model lacks per-actor limits and separation of duties. — Capability plus a published policy limit plus optional dual approval; never treat a bank permission as sufficient. — Anecdotal demand; absence of a feature in the wiki is not proof that no hidden limit exists. — AX-07. — UD-046.

**ALR-010** — *Are inactive/gray nations taxed?* — ALS-002 — Gray nations are not taxed. — Inactivity has economic semantics. — Align with dormancy (D-028) rather than inventing a second inactivity state. — Official. — AX-03, AX-06. — none.

**ALR-011** — *Are alliance treaties native objects?* — ALS-004, ALS-006 — Foreign-affairs guidance covers embassies and treaties; alliance pages publish treaty lists (MDoAP, ODoAP, NAP, ODP, protectorate, extension); no binding alliance-treaty mechanics were observed. — Treaty lists are political communication. — Do not create alliance treaty objects; see UD-039. — Absence of evidence, not proof of absence. — AX-15, AX-16. — UD-039.

**ALR-012** — *Where does diplomacy happen?* — ALS-004 — Official guidance suggests an embassy area on the alliance Discord "or somewhere else". — The game expects external venues. — Static link plus an in-game position record. — Official. — AX-16. — UD-048.

**ALR-013** — *What does P&W forbid around banks?* — ALS-005 — Evading same-network trade restrictions via alliance banks is bannable; bank protection via vacation mode or a new nation is prohibited. — Shared pools create evasion paths. — Reuse Economy restricted-pair checks; explicit prohibition. — P&W rules, analogous not identical. — AX-07. — UD-046.

**ALR-014** — *Is automated banking allowed?* — ALS-005 — Automated banking is prohibited; a bot exposing a **manual** withdrawal trigger is given as acceptable. — Automation policy is about who authorizes, not the interface. — No bots; server-side authorization only. — Official example. — AX-07. — D-133.

**ALR-015** — *Are multiple nations allowed?* — ALS-005 — One nation per player; shared-network restrictions apply to interactions. — Identity rules are foundational. — Reuse the existing one-nation rule; no alliance exception. — Official. — AX-01. — none.

**ALR-016** — *Can same-network nations share an alliance?* — ALS-005 — Yes, but they may not jointly declare on the same target; other interactions are restricted. — Alliance membership is not a trust boundary. — Economy/Military checks must be membership-aware, not membership-trusting. — Official. — AX-07, AX-18. — none.

**ALR-017** — *How are new nations restricted?* — ALS-005 — New nations may not send all or most assets away. — Anti-alt transfer rules exist. — Reuse fresh-nation rules in treasury deposits. — Official. — AX-07. — none.

**ALR-018** — *How is moderation governed?* — ALS-005 — Moderation actions require two moderators. — Dual control is a credible pattern. — Consider dual approval for destructive alliance actions. — Official. — AX-23. — UD-046.

**ALR-019** — *How much does P&W charge for alliances?* — ALS-005 — Not established in accessible sources. — No cost claim can be made. — No numeric founding cost in 09A. — Missing evidence. — AX-01. — UD-040.

**ALR-020** — *Is Discord prevalent?* — ALS-006–ALS-011, ALS-026 — Every inspected alliance publishes Discord as the application or community venue; the Black Knights page calls it the place where most communication happens. — Discord is the de facto external layer. — A static link is the correct native surface. — Selection bias toward active alliances. — AX-19. — UD-048.

**ALR-021** — *Is Discord mandatory?* — ALS-006, ALS-011, ALS-026 — Rose states Discord is mandatory; the Black Knights live page states "it is a requirement for joining the Black Knights"; other alliances strongly imply it. — Alliances may require it; the game does not. — Keep game access independent of Discord. — Alliance-specific. — AX-19. — UD-048.

**ALR-022** — *What does an alliance site add?* — ALS-011, ALS-012, ALS-026 — BK Net provides a Discord application bot with an interview workflow, plus banking, logistics and military tooling; AEGIS adds verification, pipelines and treasury tools. — Demand exists for structure the game lacks, and alliances build it outside the game. — Supply authoritative records, not workflows or bots. — Third-party tooling and alliance-owned pages; no independent reliability claim. — AX-09, AX-20. — none.

**ALR-023** — *Can permissions be customized?* — ALS-013 — Change Permissions allows creating, editing and deleting custom positions with permissions from the 15-item list; custom positions may have any positive position level. — Custom roles are expected. — Custom roles with capability bundles and audit; a custom office cannot exceed its creator's rights. — Community documentation. — AX-05. — UD-045.

**ALR-024** — *How are members removed?* — ALS-003, ALS-013 — Removal is an officer action gated by Remove Members; leaks are removed; no appeal process is documented. — Expulsion is unilateral natively. — Add notice/appeal as a candidate; do not claim P&W has one. — Guidance, not rule. — AX-09. — UD-044.

**ALR-025** — *How is succession handled?* — ALS-001, ALS-006, ALS-013 — An Heir exists; the wiki says an Heir can appoint themselves Leader when the alliance has no leader; no automatic inactivity succession is documented. — Succession is social or manual, not scheduled. — An explicit succession rule is required. — Not documented ≠ absent. — AX-04. — UD-043.

**ALR-026** — *What activity data is used?* — ALS-003 — Login activity, vacation mode, city/infrastructure audits. — Alliances monitor members. — Provide aggregate readiness only; no surveillance features. — Guidance. — AX-12. — UD-044.

**ALR-027** — *What does AEGIS model?* — ALS-012 — Departments, role templates, least-privilege "minimum necessary access", applicant isolation, grants/loans/safekeeping/offshores, approval limits, audit history. — Serious alliances build a control plane. — Adopt the principles, not the tooling. — Third-party. — AX-05, AX-07, AX-21. — UD-045, UD-046.

**ALR-028** — *How is Discord identity verified?* — ALS-012 — `/link nation_id` and `/setup` via bot; manual fallback. — Linking is a third-party convenience. — Explicitly exclude. — Third-party. — AX-19. — D-133.

**ALR-029** — *How are applicants isolated?* — ALS-012 — The applicant role sees limited channels until accepted. — Onboarding gating is external. — The native equivalent is field visibility, not channel ACLs. — Third-party. — AX-10. — UD-048.

**ALR-030** — *How is treasury misuse prevented?* — ALS-012 — Approval limits, named grants/loans, safekeeping, offshore ledgers and history. — Controls are procedural plus tooling. — A native ledger with approvals is the minimum. — Third-party. — AX-07. — UD-046.

**ALR-031** — *How is compliance checked?* — ALS-012 — MMR/warchest/activity compliance dashboards. — Alliances enforce participation. — Readiness declarations, not automated punishment. — Third-party. — AX-14. — UD-049.

**ALR-032** — *How many ranks do alliances actually use?* — ALS-011, ALS-013, ALS-016, ALS-017, ALS-026 — The native default set is three appointable levels plus Member and Applicant; a 2015 suggestion asks for more than three because the poster considered three insufficient; a 2015 bank thread calls bank access "all or nothing"; live BK uses a six-title government. There is no reliable census of how many ranks "most" alliances use. — Role proliferation varies; the earlier "most alliances use five ranks" claim was not supported and is withdrawn. — Ship a small default set and allow custom offices. — Anecdotal and dated forum evidence. — AX-05. — UD-041.

**ALR-033** — *How does eRepublik handle alliance membership?* — ALS-018, ALS-019 — An alliance leader invites a country; a law is voted by country presidents; leaving is proposed by the president and voted by congress. — Collective membership changes are politically ratified. — Consider ratification for alliance-level acts, not for individual membership. — Official wiki. — AX-16. — UD-047.

**ALR-034** — *How are MPPs approved?* — ALS-020 — President proposes; cost is paid from each treasury; congress votes. — Treaties carry cost and ratification. — Any binding diplomacy needs consent plus cost accounting. — Official wiki. — AX-16. — UD-039.

**ALR-035** — *Can military leaders seize power?* — ALS-021 — A military-unit commander can coup the unit. — Leadership capture is a designed risk. — Explicit removal/succession rules. — Official wiki. — AX-18. — UD-043.

**ALR-036** — *How do federations handle leadership?* — ALS-022 — Federation types, rotating presidency, cohesion, laws, federal fleets. — Rotation and law levels limit capture. — Evaluate rotation as an option, not a default. — Indirect access. — AX-05. — UD-041, UD-042.

**ALR-037** — *What did 08A find about EVE?* — ALS-023 — Office/role authority, war mechanics following membership changes, player-made standings. — Role revocation and membership-change semantics matter. — Reuse the "membership change does not silently rewrite treaties" principle. — Secondary. — AX-15. — none.

**ALR-038** — *What did 08A find about Call of War?* — ALS-023 — Coalition coordination without ownership transfer. — Coordination ≠ control. — Same boundary as military. — Secondary, thin. — AX-18. — none.

**ALR-039** — *What native communication exists?* — ALS-001–ALS-006 — Alliance page text, announcements/description, applicant list; no chat, DM, presence or read receipts observed. — P&W itself does not provide chat. — Confirms the owner's model is consistent with the comparator. — Absence of evidence. — AX-19. — D-133.

**ALR-040** — *What is the cheapest sufficient native set?* — synthesis — Profile, membership, roles/capabilities, asynchronous notices, treasury ledger, static link. — These cover the observed legitimate needs. — Adopt as candidate scope. — Synthesis/judgement. — all. — UD-049.

**ALR-041** — *What is the complete current P&W permission inventory?* — ALS-013 — The community wiki lists exactly **15** permissions: View Bank; Withdraw Bank; Change Permissions; See Spies; See Reset Timers; Tax Brackets; Post Announcements; Manage Announcements; Manage Applicants; Remove Members; Edit Alliance Info; Manage Treaties; Manage Market Share; Manage Embargoes; Promote Self to Leader. — The earlier research described only a subset and understated the inventory. — Document all 15 and do not copy the bundle. — Community documentation; no official matrix; no revision date. — AX-05, AX-07, AX-16, AX-19, AX-20. — UD-045.

**ALR-042** — *How are permissions bundled by default?* — ALS-013 — Leader is unrestricted; Heir has "all the same powers as leaders" and can self-appoint when no Leader exists; Officer holds View Bank, Change Permissions, Post/Manage Announcements, Edit Alliance Info, Manage Treaties, Manage Applicants and Remove Members; Member has no powers; Applicant has no powers and does not count in statistics. — The default Officer bundle combines membership control, treaty control, profile control and permission control. — Nation Supremacy must separate these capabilities and must not make `roles.manage` a default officer power. — Community documentation. — AX-04, AX-05, AX-16, AX-20. — UD-045.

**ALR-043** — *Can a custom position carry any permission?* — ALS-013 — A holder of Change Permissions can create, edit and delete custom positions "with permissions they define from the list above"; the page recommends least privilege and specifically warns to be cautious who receives Promote Self to Leader. — Custom positions can combine arbitrary permissions, including self-promotion. — A custom office must not be able to exceed the creator's own rights; granting `roles.manage` must not be bundled with ordinary office creation. — Community documentation; the page does not say whether a holder can assign a custom position to themselves. — AX-05. — UD-045.

**ALR-044** — *What does Promote Self to Leader permit?* — ALS-013, ALS-027 — The permission lets a nation "promote themselves to the Leader position at any time"; the wiki warns about granting it. The Heir position can also self-appoint when the alliance has no leader. A 2018 forum suggestion discusses self-promotion and self-demotion, showing community concern about rank self-changes. — This is a designed leadership-capture path, not a hypothetical. — Nation Supremacy must not copy it; leadership transfer must require an explicit, audited procedure and should require confirmation/re-authentication. — Community documentation plus anecdotal forum evidence; no incident data. — AX-04, AX-05. — UD-043, UD-045.

**ALR-045** — *How are treasury permissions separated?* — ALS-013 — View Bank and Withdraw Bank are separate; Withdraw Bank moves resources to nations or other alliances; the inventory has no approve, limit or dual-control permission; tax brackets are a third capability. — P&W separates seeing from withdrawing but not proposing from approving. — Split view, deposit, propose, approve and execute; require a published limit and optional dual approval. — Community documentation. — AX-06, AX-07. — UD-046.

**ALR-046** — *How do treaty and embargo permissions compare with national authority?* — ALS-013 — Manage Treaties lets a holder "send, accept, or reject treaties"; Manage Embargoes lets a holder "place or remove embargoes on behalf of your alliance". These are alliance-level powers in P&W. — Nation Supremacy's B-08 gives the nation owner sole diplomatic authority and refuses delegation to an alliance office. — These P&W permissions are evidence of a different model, not a rule to copy; an alliance position may be published, but treaty consent and embargo imposition remain national acts. — Community documentation; the evidence does not establish binding alliance-wide treaty effects. — AX-16, AX-17. — UD-039, UD-047.

**ALR-047** — *What does Tax Brackets permit, and should it be copied?* — ALS-002, ALS-013 — The permission lets a holder see, edit and delete tax brackets and move members between them; P&W collects monetary and resource percentages automatically, with a Default bracket; gray nations are exempt. — Member tax exposure can be changed by one permission holder, with no native approval step documented. — Do not invent a Nation Supremacy taxation model; automatic taxation remains excluded from the first release (`REQ-578`). Any future tax needs an Economy decision and member-visible rules. — Official manual plus community documentation. — AX-06. — UD-046.

**ALR-048** — *What is the Black Knights recruitment process in 2026?* — ALS-011, ALS-026 — Three verified stages: (1) become an Applicant in-game through the official alliance join screen, with the nation appearing in Black Knights at the Applicant position; (2) join the alliance Discord, which the live page states is a requirement and which owns the application identity; (3) run `/apply nationid:<your nation ID>` in Discord, after which the bot creates an interview workflow and `/applications status` reports progress. The bot is an alliance-operated third-party tool, not a P&W feature. — The process is a hybrid of native applicant state and external Discord/bot workflow. — Nation Supremacy records the applicant state and outcome natively; external interview steps remain optional alliance practice. — Live alliance page plus alliance-owned site; no independent verification of acceptance criteria. — AX-09, AX-19. — UD-044, UD-048.

**ALR-049** — *Are Black Knights' minimum requirements and review times published?* — ALS-011 — The "Requirements and policies" panel contains unpublished placeholder text ("Tell applicants what kind of alliance they are joining...", "List the minimum requirements...", "Say how long reviews usually take..."). No minimum nation age, city count, activity rule or review duration is published. — The earlier report's claim of a 24-hour or timed process described Rose, not Black Knights; it must not be transferred. — Do not assert BK thresholds or timelines; describe only the three verified stages and the interview workflow. — The section is a CMS placeholder, not an alliance policy. — AX-09. — UD-044.
