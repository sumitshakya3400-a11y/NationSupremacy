**Checksum convention.** Each SHA-256 is computed over the exact bytes of the archived file as stored (UTF-8). The R6 and R7 archives use LF (`\n`) newlines (0 CRLF); reproduce with `python -c "import hashlib,pathlib;print(hashlib.sha256(pathlib.Path('<file>').read_bytes()).hexdigest())"` or `sha256sum <file>`. No newline normalization is applied to the recorded digest; the digest is of the stored bytes.

**Scope note.** The archive preserves the original Prompt 12B instruction (required by 12B-C1) and the operative 12B-C1 and 12B-C1-R1 corrective instructions (preserved by 12B-C1-R1). Earlier phase instructions (Prompts 01–12A) were not supplied as recoverable verbatim text in this session and are **not** reconstructed here; their execution records remain in the progress log and the stage reports. An interrupted first draft of the 12B-C1 instruction was superseded by the operative version and is not archived. **Prompt 12C is archived as of 2026-11-10 (Prompt 12C-FINAL-C1).** The Prompt 12C pass declined to archive it because the stored text could not be byte-verified against an external original at that time; the reconciliation pass archived the session-supplied instruction and states that limitation openly in the archive header and in its index row, because no independent external copy exists anywhere in the repository or in `.cloud-attachments/`. The archived digest identifies the stored bytes, not an independently verified external original, and that distinction is the record. **Prompt 12C-FINAL-C1 is a separate bounded repository-integrity instruction and is deliberately not archived as Prompt 12C**; its scope, authorizations and results are recorded in [the reconciliation inventory](../qa_backups/12C_FINAL_C1_conflict_reconciliation.md) and [stage 2 progress log §86](../stage-2/06_stage_2_progress_log.md).
